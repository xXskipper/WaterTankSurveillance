Bill of Materials – Water Tank Surveillance System
===================================================

2x  Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 V4
      Chip      : ESP32-S3R2
      LoRa chip : SX1262
      Display   : 0.96" OLED (SSD1306, 128x64, I2C)
      Frequency : 868 MHz (EU)
      URL       : https://heltec.org/project/wifi-lora-32-v4/

2x  AZDelivery Polysilicon Mini Solar Panel
      Voltage : 5 V
      Power   : 1.5 W
      Notes   : Waterproof resin encapsulated

2x  3.7 V 3000 mAh LiPo Battery
      Connector : JST 1.25
      Protection: Built-in PCB
      Compatible with Heltec LoRa 32 JST connector

1x  ARCELI JSN-SR04T Waterproof Ultrasonic Distance Sensor
      Range    : 20 – 600 cm
      VCC      : 5 V
      Trigger  : 10 µs pulse on TRIG pin
      Echo     : HIGH pulse proportional to distance
      Notes    : IP67 suitable for outdoor/wet environments

===================================================
Pin Mapping – Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 V4 (ESP32-S3)
===================================================

LoRa (SX1262):
  SCK   → GPIO 9
  MISO  → GPIO 11
  MOSI  → GPIO 10
  CS    → GPIO 8
  RST   → GPIO 12
  DIO1  → GPIO 14
  BUSY  → GPIO 13

OLED (SSD1306, I2C):
  SDA   → GPIO 17
  SCL   → GPIO 18
  RST   → GPIO 21
  Addr  → 0x3C

Sensor (JSN-SR04T) – Sender board only:
  TRIG  → GPIO 4
  ECHO  → GPIO 5

Button (PRG/BOOT):
  Pin   → GPIO 0  (active LOW)
  Use   → Triggers manual display readout + link check on sender
